Questions.

How is this different from Langfuse or Helicone?

They’re observability tools. They show what a user cost, but they don’t know what that user pays you. MarginCap joins the two and gives you margin.

Can’t Stripe’s token billing do this?

Stripe token billing helps you charge customers for tokens. If you’re on flat or per-seat pricing, like most of us, it doesn’t show which customers you’re losing money on.

Do I need usage-based pricing?

No. MarginCap is built for flat and per-seat pricing, which is exactly where power users hide.

What if I don’t use Stripe?

You still get cost per customer and hard caps. A CSV revenue import is coming soon.
