How it works

Three steps. About five minutes.

01

Wrap your client

One line around the SDK you already use.

track(new OpenAI(), {
  customerId: stripeId,
  feature: "summarize",
})
02

Connect Stripe

Read-only access. We pull revenue per customer and touch nothing else.

03

See your margin table

Revenue, AI cost and margin for every customer. Money-losers sorted to the top.

What you get

Know who's profitable. Stop who isn't.

Margin per customer

Revenue minus AI cost for every Stripe customer, with a 30-day trend.

Cost per feature

See which feature eats the budget: "summarize", "chat", that agent loop.

Alerts

Slack or email when a customer's AI cost passes X% of what they pay, or spikes 3× overnight.

Hard caps

Budget alerts tell you after the money is gone. canSpend(customerId) returns allow, warn or block before the call, so one runaway loop can't eat your month.

Privacy

We never see your prompts or completions.

The SDK sends token counts, model, customer ID and feature tag. Nothing else. It runs async, outside your request path, so it can't slow down or break your app.

FAQ

Questions

How is this different from Langfuse or Helicone?

They're observability tools. They show what a user cost, but they don't know what that user pays you. MarginCap joins the two and gives you margin.

Can't Stripe's token billing do this?

Stripe token billing helps you charge customers for tokens. If you're on flat or per-seat pricing, like most of us, it doesn't show which customers you're losing money on.

Do I need usage-based pricing?

No. MarginCap is built for flat and per-seat pricing, which is exactly where power users hide.

What if I don't use Stripe?

You still get cost per customer and hard caps. A CSV revenue import is coming soon.
