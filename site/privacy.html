Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy covers the pre-launch MarginCap website (margincap.dev): the waitlist and the founding-member pre-order. MarginCap itself is still in development; this policy will be updated, and waitlist members emailed, before the product launches.

Who we are

MarginCap is operated by its founder, an independent developer. The operator's full legal name and business address will be listed here before paid plans go live. Contact: hello@margincap.dev.

We don't collect sensitive data (health, biometrics, government IDs, precise location). Card numbers go directly to Stripe; we never see or store them. We don't use AI models on your data.

Cookies and similar technologies (cookie policy)

  • margincap.dev sets no cookies. No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and no tracking pixels.
  • If you join the waitlist, your browser stores one item in local storage, mc_waitlist: a one-way SHA-256 hash of your email, so the form can tell you you're already signed up. It contains no readable email, never leaves your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings at any time. It is strictly necessary for that feature, so it doesn't require consent.
  • If you click a pre-order button, you go to Stripe's checkout page, which sets its own cookies for fraud prevention under Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Analytics: we don't currently run any analytics. If we add Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookieless, no cross-site tracking), we will update this section before switching it on.
  • Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control: we don't track you across sites, so there is nothing for these signals to switch off.

International transfers

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where EU/UK data leaves those regions, it is protected by the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

  • Waitlist emails: until 12 months after launch, or until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Payment records: as long as tax law requires (typically 6–7 years).
  • Server logs: as kept by Cloudflare under its policy.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, to stop using it, or object to how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@margincap.dev from the address you signed up with; we reply within 30 days. Deleting your data also cancels and refunds any pre-order. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe option.

If you're unhappy with our answer you can complain to your data protection authority: in the EU your national authority, in the UK the ICO, in Canada the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

MarginCap is a business tool and isn't directed at children under 16. We don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date and email waitlist members before the change applies.
