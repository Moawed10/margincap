Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

MarginCap is built and run by one independent developer. This page covers the pre-launch website at margincap.dev. It will be updated before the product itself launches.

What we collect

  • Your email, if you join the waitlist, plus the link you arrived from (for example "reddit"), so we know which channels work.
  • Payment details, if you pre-order. These go directly to Stripe; we never see or store your card number. We receive your name, email, country and payment status from Stripe.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (page views, load performance) via Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not track you across sites.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The site stores one item in your browser (whether you've already joined the waitlist) so you don't sign up twice. It never leaves your device.

Who processes your data

  • Formspree — stores waitlist signups.
  • Stripe — processes pre-order payments.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the site and provides cookieless analytics.

How long we keep it

Waitlist emails are kept until launch plus 12 months, or until you ask us to delete them. Payment records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your rights: access, correction, deletion

Email hello@margincap.dev from the address you signed up with and we'll send you your data, correct it, or delete it within 30 days. Deleting your data also cancels any pre-order and refunds it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll email people on the waitlist before it takes effect.
